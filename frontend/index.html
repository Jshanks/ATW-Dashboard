Failover group preview

Recommendations spread Primary canaries across unique host IPs where possible.

What are canaries? Primary canaries are a small, representative group of Warriors that remain on the primary project during a manual failover. Their continued activity helps show whether the primary project has recovered while the rest of the assigned fleet runs the fallback project.

The dashboard recommends 5% of eligible Warriors, with a minimum of 3 and maximum of 10. It prioritizes different host IPs so one host or network failure is less likely to distort the signal.

Review before saving: IP diversity is only an approximation of infrastructure diversity. Multiple IPs can still share a physical host, ISP, VPN, storage system, or other failure domain. Prefer stable Warriors distributed across genuinely separate hosts and locations where known.
